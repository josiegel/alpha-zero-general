Score in training games

Game length

Average decisions per finished game, both players together.

Policy entropy

How spread out the bot's move choices are. It should fall slowly; a crash toward 0 means it stopped exploring.

Value loss

Error of the bot's win prediction (mean squared; results are +1 or −1). Lower means it judges positions better.

Policy change per update

Approximate KL divergence between the policy before and after each update. Steady values around 0.01 are healthy.
